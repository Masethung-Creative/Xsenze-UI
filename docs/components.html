Form validation

Add data-xz-validate to a form to check it on submit with the browser's own rules (required, type, minlength, pattern). Errors appear under each field in plain language, focus moves to the first one, and each field re-checks as the person fixes it.

At least 8 characters.

    Indian grouping puts a comma after every two digits once past the thousands, so 12,34,567 is twelve lakh, thirty-four thousand, five hundred and sixty-seven.
